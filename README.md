# INVESTIFY_investment_got_simplified

# 📈 INVESTIFY — Investment Made Simplified

> **A real-time, multi-agent AI financial analytics dashboard built to bridge retail trading insights, institutional order-flow sentiment, and sovereign fixed-income comparisons.**

---

## 🌟 Executive Summary

**INVESTIFY** is a comprehensive wealth intelligence platform designed to simplify complex market dynamics. By synthesizing technical price action, audited regulatory filings (via RAG), institutional order flows, and fixed-income assets into a unified dashboard, INVESTIFY provides both retail investors and aggressive traders with institutional-grade decision support.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* **🤖 Autonomous Multi-Agent AI Engine:**
  * **Agent 1 (Technical & Trend):** Monitors price vectors, 20-Day SMA, volume anomalies, RSI, and support/resistance zones.
  * **Agent 2 (Fundamental & SEBI RAG):** Queries audited SEBI filings, earnings call transcripts, and regulatory risk factors.
  * **Agent 3 (Institutional Flow):** Tracks FII/DII net flows, Options Put-Call Ratio (PCR), and order-book depth sentiment.
  * **Consensus Matrix:** Combines agent signals into a weighted overall score ($88/100$) and master trade verdict.

* **🕯️ Interactive Technical & Volume Breakdown:**
  * Separated high-precision OHLC Candlestick and Volume charts using Plotly.
  * Custom timeframe filtering (`1D`, `1Y`, `5Y`, `LIFETIME`).

* **💰 Wealth Growth & Compounding SIP Engine:**
  * Real compounding calculation formula for Systematic Investment Plans ($FV = P \times \frac{(1 + r)^n - 1}{r} \times (1 + r)$).
  * Year-by-year stacked visual breakdown comparing invested principal vs estimated wealth gains over 1 to 30 years.

* **🏛 Sovereign & Corporate Fixed-Income Portal:**
  * Live comparison matrix between 10Y Benchmark G-Secs, T-Bills, SGBs, SDLs, and AAA Corporate Bonds.
  * Interactive Yield Curve visualization and fixed-income return calculator.

* **👁 Real-Time Market Watchlist:**
  * Vertical scanner monitoring active NIFTY 50 constituents with instant single-click dashboard routing.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

* **Frontend & UI:** Python, Streamlit, Custom HTML5/CSS3 (Webslinger Neon Theme & Dynamic Micro-interactions)
* **Data Visualization:** Plotly Graph Objects, Pandas, NumPy
* **AI & Document Intelligence:** Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) Architecture, Multi-Agent Consensus Matrix
* **Market Data Pipeline:** Automated Index & Equity Loader Engine
